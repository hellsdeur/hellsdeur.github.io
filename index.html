I work on global continuous optimization, data mining, and natural language processing, with applied projects built in partnership with public institutions in the State of Pará, Brazil.

Research interests

  • Continuous global optimization: population-based metaheuristics, swarm intelligence, benchmark evaluation on hard continuous landscapes, and runtime analysis.
  • Data mining: knowledge discovery in large databases, associative rule learning, and pattern discovery in real transactional and institutional data.
  • Natural language processing: text classification, language models, and retrieval-augmented pipelines applied to institutional records.
  • Applied machine learning: supervised models for public security, education, retail, and healthcare settings.
